Privacy Policy

iM Time Command · Last Updated: August 18, 2026

Summary: Your data belongs to you. iM Time Command stores 100% of your agenda planner events, collaborator clocks, messaging recaps, and contact list locally inside your web browser / device. We do not operate remote tracking servers or sell your data.

1. Local Storage Architecture

All operational data created within iM Time Command — including partner clocks, working hour thresholds, agenda events, growth XP scores, and client vault notes — is serialized directly to your device's localStorage and IndexedDB. No personal user data is ever transmitted to or stored on external servers hosted by iM Time Command.

2. Device Contact Access (`navigator.contacts`)

When you utilize the optional "Import Phone Contacts" feature, the application uses the browser's native Web Contact Picker API (navigator.contacts.select). This picker operates entirely under your explicit control:

  • The app only accesses specific contacts you explicitly select in the native system drawer.
  • Contact details (Name, Phone, Email) are processed locally on your device to resolve time zones.
  • Contact information is never uploaded to any remote server or third party.

3. Google API Services (Gmail & Google Calendar)

iM Time Command offers optional integration with Google API Services (OAuth 2.0) to schedule Google Calendar events and send Gmail reminders. If enabled by the user:

  • OAuth access tokens are cached locally in your browser session only.
  • Google Calendar event URLs are constructed client-side via standard Google templates.
  • iM Time Command's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Native App Deep Linking

When connecting with collaborators across WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger, Instagram, LinkedIn, or Signal, the application launches custom native URI schemes (e.g., whatsapp://, tg://). Communication occurs directly through your installed third-party applications according to their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Export & Permanent Erasure

You maintain full control over your data at all times. Using the in-app controls in the footer, you can:

  • Export Backup: Download your entire workspace state as a JSON backup file at any time.
  • Restore Backup: Load a previously exported JSON backup onto any browser or device.
  • Reset All: Instantly wipe all cached clocks, agenda events, and saved preferences from your device.
